Network Architecture Principles
Future forest-scale mesh topology & operational design
check
Cluster-Based Full Mesh: Forest is partitioned into overlapping cells (25–50 nodes, ~20–60 km² each) eliminating radio shadows and coverage dead zones.
check
Edge Gateways with Backhaul: High-reliability gateways (4G / Fiber / Satellite) positioned along accessible forest borders — no fragile central servers deep in the canopy.
check
Multi-Hop Dynamic Routing: Alerts relay hop-to-hop (4–6 hops max) to perimeter gateways; self-healing mesh dynamically bypasses damaged or depleted nodes.
check
Every Node a Transceiver: Dual-capability nodes execute on-device TinyML audio inference while serving as intermediate LoRa mesh packet repeaters.
check
Independent Scaling: Physical seminar prototype proves the 2-node LoRa link; the multi-hop routing protocol scales seamlessly to thousands of square kilometers.
info
Status & Scope Clarification
Illustrative engineering design only. Demonstrates reserve-scale viability. Actual node spacing (~250–400 m) and canopy attenuation will be calibrated through field RSSI/SNR trials; the physical PS2 demonstrator remains the committed 2-node prototype.
