Defined Project Scope

Development and physical bench validation of a 2-node solar-powered acoustic sensor prototype coupled to an off-grid Raspberry Pi LoRaWAN gateway and real-time ranger dashboard.
Encompasses embedded hardware integration, custom I2S audio acquisition, on-device 7-class TinyML classification, ultra-lean LoRaWAN (IN865) telemetry, and dual-layer visualization (Instant Threat Map + Long-term Soundscape Health Index).
flag

Specific Research & Implementation Objectives

1
Architect and fabricate an ultra-low-cost (~₹9,260), solar-powered 2-node prototype based on ESP32-S3 and INMP441 MEMS mic with battery autonomy.
2
Train, optimize, and deploy a quantized INT8 7-class CNN (<70 KB footprint) achieving <250 ms on-device audio inference.
3
Establish a license-exempt LoRaWAN IN865 telemetry uplink from nodes to a Raspberry Pi 4 + SX1276 receiver gateway running ChirpStack v4.
4
Deploy an interactive ranger dashboard providing <10s SMS alerts via Fast2SMS, biophony heatmaps, and continuous Forest Health Index tracking.
5
Execute empirical field validation across varying canopy ranges (20 m / 50 m / 100 m) to measure detection accuracy, SNR attenuation, and packet delivery ratio.
