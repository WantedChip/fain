3Experimental Evaluation of a LoRa Wildlife Monitoring Network in a Forest Vegetation Area Ojo
2021		 Empirically evaluate LoRa signal propagation and network performance for wildlife monitoring within a dense forest vegetation environment. Field trials measuring RSSI, SNR, and packet delivery ratio at varying distances and node placements within forest canopy cover, using commercial LoRa transceivers. Found significant signal attenuation from foliage, with reliable range considerably shorter than open-field LoRa specifications; identified optimal antenna placement strategies. Limited to a single forest type and season; does not address adaptive range-optimization strategies across varying canopy density or seasonal foliage change.
4The Remote Environmental Assessment Laboratory's Acoustic Library (NDSI) Kasten
2012		 Establish an acoustic archive and introduce the Normalized Difference Soundscape Index (NDSI) for quantifying biophony-to-anthrophony ratios in soundscape ecology. Long-term acoustic recordings analyzed using frequency-band energy ratios to compute NDSI as a proxy for ecosystem health and human disturbance. NDSI successfully differentiated relatively undisturbed soundscapes from human-impacted ones across multiple sites, establishing it as a standard soundscape ecology metric. Index degrades under rain/wind noise contamination; requires ML-assisted filtering for reliable use in noisy or highly variable field conditions.
