Novelty / Innovation
•
Combines four elements no existing system integrates together: on-device 7-class TinyML inference, LoRaWAN telemetry (no cellular SIM), solar energy autonomy, and dual threat + biodiversity output.
•
Unlike De Miguel et al. (2025), which streams compressed audio to the cloud for classification, FAIN performs 100% of inference on-device, transmitting only a 10–16 byte packet per event.
Methodology (Phase-wise Execution)
1
Dataset collection
ESC-50, UrbanSound8K, Freesound, Xeno-canto + custom recordings (3,600+ clips across 7 classes).
2
Feature extraction
16 kHz audio → 512-pt FFT → 40-bin Mel filterbank → MFCC (40×101).
3
CNN training
CNN training (~186K params) and INT8 quantization to a <70 KB deployable model.
4
Embedded deployment
TFLite Micro on ESP32-S3 (80 KB tensor arena, <250 ms inference).
5
Firmware logic
Threat-alert path (>0.85 confidence) plus a 60-second ecological heartbeat path.
6
Field testing
Field testing and validation at 20 m / 50 m / 100 m distances in diverse vegetation.
