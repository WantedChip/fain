Core Purpose
Why We Are Building FAIN
To build a solar-powered, 2-node acoustic sensor prototype that autonomously flags illegal forest activity (chainsaws, gunshots, blasts, forest fires) in real time while continuously measuring ecosystem health via bird-chorus tracking — operating completely independently of cellular infrastructure.

Ecological Background
The Immediate Conservation Gap
India loses roughly 1.5 million hectares of forest cover each year. Existing surveillance tools fail: satellites detect damage only days after felling occurs, camera traps suffer high false-trigger rates and narrow fields of view, and manual foot patrols cannot cover dense, roadless forest reserves.

Technical Approach
How FAIN Solves The Challenge
Continuous audio sampling via I2S MEMS mic → on-device INT8 TinyML inference (7-class CNN) on ESP32-S3 → instant LoRaWAN (IN865) packet transmission to a Raspberry Pi 4 gateway → ranger dashboard featuring SMS alerts and a soundscape Health Index.
