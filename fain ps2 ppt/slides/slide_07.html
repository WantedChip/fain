5Fighting Poaching Through Real-Time Gunshot Detection with Multi-Sensor SAIL Verification Dhar
2025		 Develop a real-time gunshot detection system for anti-poaching applications, using multi-sensor cross-verification to reduce false alarms. Acoustic gunshot signatures classified via machine learning and cross-verified across multiple sensor nodes (SAIL) before triggering an alert; tested against known false-positive sources. Multi-sensor corroboration substantially reduced false-positive alert rates compared to single-sensor detection, while maintaining high true-positive gunshot detection. Assumes dense multi-sensor coverage per zone; class imbalance between rare gunshot events and abundant ambient sound remains a persistent training challenge.
6An Efficient and Low-Cost Realization of LoRa-Based Real-Time Forest Protection System Acharya
2024		 Design and deploy a low-cost, solar-powered, LoRa-based real-time forest protection system for an Indian wildlife sanctuary. PIR-motion and amplitude-threshold sensor nodes transmit alerts over LoRa to a central gateway; system deployed and field-tested at Eturnagaram Wildlife Sanctuary, Telangana. Demonstrated a functional, affordable LoRa-based alert system for an Indian forest deployment, validating solar-powered LoRa nodes as a low-cost surveillance option. Relies on simple thresholding with no neural/ML classification, leading to high false-trigger rates from non-threat motion and foliage noise.
